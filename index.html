Speak with your eyes.

Orien is a free iPhone app for people who can't speak or use their hands. Look toward a word or phrase, and your iPhone says it out loud.

How it works

Every screen in Orien has at most four big choices, one at each edge. Look toward one and hold. That's all there is to learn.

  1. Set up the phoneMount your iPhone in front of your face. Orien uses the same front camera as Face ID, so there's nothing extra to buy.

  2. Look toward a choiceLook up, down, left or right to highlight what you want to say.

  3. Hold your gaze or blinkOrien picks it, and your iPhone says it out loud.

Why four directions

  • A phone camera can tell up, down, left and right apart without a calibration step, so there are no small buttons to aim at.
  • The same four moves work on every screen. Inside a menu, look down to go back.
  • The middle of the screen picks nothing, so you can rest your eyes there.

What you can say and do

Ask for help fast

"Help me," "I'm in pain" and "Stop" are right up front, a glance away.

Needs and feelings

Food, drink, comfort and care, plus how you feel, including showing where it hurts.

Talk to the people you love

Say their name and "Can you come here?", "Please stay with me" or "I love you." Get texts ready to send.

Say anything

Spell your own words with a keyboard you control with your eyes.

Photos and videos

Look through your photos and watch your videos without touching the screen.

Built for long days

The screen stays on while Orien is open. If the camera loses your face, Orien pauses and says so, instead of guessing.

Make it fit you

There's no calibration step. Open Orien and start talking, then fine tune it in Settings, on the left of the third page. You can change every setting with your eyes or by tapping.

Speed
Standard, Fast, Expert or Instant. Standard waits 3 seconds before it opens a menu, which gives the most time to change your mind. Move up as it gets easier.
Sensitivity
How far the gaze dot on the keyboard moves when your eyes move. Side to side and up and down are set separately, so it can match eyes that move more easily one way than the other.
Voice
Any English voice installed on your iPhone.
Tutorial
Replay the guided tour any time.
Touch
Caregivers can tap anything, any time. A tapped phrase is spoken right away.

What you need

  • An iPhone with Face ID running iOS 17 or later. That's iPhone XR, XS or any newer model with Face ID. iPhone SE models won't work.
  • A mount or stand that holds the phone steady in front of the face.
  • Good light on the face. A bright window behind the person makes tracking harder.

Orien also runs on iPad, in a window the size of a phone. Orien checks your iPad when it opens and tells you if it can't follow your eyes. A full screen iPad version is on the way.

Your privacy

Everything stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers. The camera is used only to follow your eyes, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. We can't see anything you do in Orien.

For clinicians and speech therapists

Orien is a bridge, not a replacement. A dedicated eye gaze device and an AAC evaluation are still the right path for many people, and they serve people a phone camera can't.

Because Orien is free, a family can start today, while they wait on a device or on insurance. If you'd like to try it with patients, or tell us what's missing, we'd love to hear from you.

Common questions

Is Orien really free?

Yes. Every way to communicate in Orien is free, and that won't change. Later on there may be optional extras that cost money to run, like sending texts without touching the phone. If that happens, they'll be clearly labeled, and they'll never lock the ways you communicate.

Does Orien record my face?

No. Orien reads where your eyes are pointing, on your phone, and throws the camera image away right after. No video, photos or face data is saved or sent anywhere.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Orien is built for iPhone, and on iPad it runs in a window the size of a phone. A full screen iPad version is planned for a future update.

Can I use Orien in an emergency?

No. Orien is a communication aid, not an emergency system. In an emergency, call 911 or use Emergency SOS on your iPhone.

Something isn't working. Where can I get help?

Visit the help page or send us a message. We read every message.
